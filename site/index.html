Install on your phone

KhaanaBuddy is a direct APK download — here's how to get it running in under a minute.

1
Download the APKTap the "Download APK" button above. It saves to your Downloads folder.
2
Allow unknown appsAndroid will ask permission. Go to Settings → Security → "Install unknown apps" and allow your browser (Chrome, etc.).
3
Open the fileOpen Downloads, tap KhaanaBuddy.apk and hit "Install".
4
Lance it 🚀Tap "Open", sign in with Google, and start cooking.

⚠️ Your phone may show a Play Protect or "unknown source" warning — that's normal for direct APK installs. The app is safe; just confirm to continue. On some Android 14+ phones, if the install is blocked from the browser, open the APK from your Files app instead.
